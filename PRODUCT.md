# East Grinstead Arcade

## Platform and players

Free browser arcade adventures for East Grinstead locals and arcade fans. They use familiar streets, shops and events as settings. The game runs from a static host with keyboard and touch controls.

| Surface | Mode | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `docs/index.html` | Persuade | Discover the arcade and its games |
| `docs/the-big-reveal/index.html` | Read | See the story, boss artwork and real gameplay, then play |
| `docs/play/index.html` | Experience | Choose Lizzy or George and play the game |

The Big Reveal is playable across eight High Street sections. The Dunnings Music Festival is a teaser without a release date. [The game plan](GAME_PLAN.md) covers the playable episode.
