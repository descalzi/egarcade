# Grinstead Arcade

## Platform and stack

Browser game delivered as self-contained HTML, CSS and Canvas JavaScript. The encounter opens from disk or from a static host.

## Players and scene

Local players and arcade fans can try a short encounter on a laptop or a touch screen. The evening High Street sets the dark theme.

| Surface | Mode | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prototype/index.html` | Experience | Choose a hero and play the Benchy Cafe encounter |

## Scope and assets

The playable milestone covers one street scene and two waves. [The game plan](GAME_PLAN.md) covers the full episode. The cover uses the Benchy Cafe style frame. The playable street uses a separate background, with reusable hero poses and CC0 hit and breakable effects. The shopfronts are stylised from the reference photos.
